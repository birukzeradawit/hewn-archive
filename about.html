About

Heritage work, rooted in Ethiopia.

The people, principles, and story behind HEWN Tech.

Helen Gigar Tesfaye Manager

Helen Gigar Tesfaye is a civil engineer and heritage-focused professional working at the intersection of cultural heritage, conservation, research, documentation, technology, and sustainable development.

Her work brings together technical knowledge, heritage research, and archival documentation, exploring how reliable evidence can support historical study as well as heritage conservation, restoration, management, education, and future planning. It is rooted in Ethiopia's archaeological, architectural, historical, and cultural heritage.

Historical photographs, drawings, publications, archival records, field notes, maps, and oral histories provide the evidence needed to understand the history, condition, and significance of heritage places and objects — and to connect that evidence to conservation practice.

Documentation is at the foundation of this approach: organized systems for identifying, recording, describing, and preserving information, so that knowledge is easier to discover and use, and less likely to become lost or disconnected from its original context.

About HEWN Tech

Connecting heritage, knowledge, technology, conservation, and people for the future.

HEWN Tech brings together projects, research, documentation, archives, exhibitions, and collaboration.

Vision. To create a future where heritage and knowledge are preserved, accessible, valued, and shared.

Mission. To connect people, knowledge, and technology to support heritage preservation, research, conservation, learning, and innovation.

Goals

Origin

My Inspiration

A daughter's tribute

This journey began with my father, Gigar Tesfaye. Growing up, I remember the excitement I felt whenever he returned from his fieldwork. I watched him work tirelessly throughout his life — always reading, writing, researching, and documenting. His dedication gradually became part of my own childhood and, eventually, my own interest.

As I grew older, I began reading the work he had written and discovering the depth of his contribution to Ethiopian archaeology, history, culture, and heritage research. I came to understand that his work was more than a profession: he was documenting Ethiopia's heritage and civilization so that his people and future generations could know, understand, and value their history.

My respect and love for his work inspired me to begin this journey — to preserve what he left behind, make his knowledge more accessible, and build on the values he devoted his life to. HEWN Tech grew from this beginning: from a daughter's love and respect for her father's work, and a commitment to carry knowledge forward for future generations.
