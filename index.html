HEWN TECH

Preserving Ethiopia's Heritage and Knowledge

Preserving Knowledge. Connecting the Future.

HEWN Tech brings together research, documentation, archives, conservation, and technology to preserve Ethiopia's rich heritage and make it accessible for future generations. From ancient manuscripts to modern digital preservation, we bridge the gap between past and future.

Archival Documentation Digital Preservation Research Conservation Capacity Building
"Preserve the past. Understand the present. Inspire the future." — HEWN Tech Philosophy

Ancient Manuscripts

Documenting and preserving Ethiopia's centuries-old Ge'ez manuscripts, religious texts, and illuminated manuscripts that tell the story of African civilization.

Archaeological Heritage

Recording and protecting archaeological sites including Aksum's stelae, Lalibela's rock-hewn churches, and ancient monuments that span millennia.

Digital Innovation

Leveraging cutting-edge technology to create digital archives, making rare materials accessible to researchers, students, and communities worldwide.

A Platform for Knowledge

Explore projects, archives, research, exhibitions, and learning opportunities—connecting people, information, and technology to advance Ethiopian heritage preservation. Our platform serves as a bridge between traditional knowledge systems and modern digital access.
