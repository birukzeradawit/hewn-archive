Origin

My Inspiration

A daughter's tribute

This journey began with my father, Gigar Tesfaye. Growing up, I remember the excitement I felt whenever he returned from his fieldwork — always reading, writing, researching, and documenting.

My respect and love for his work inspired me to begin this journey — to preserve what he left behind, make his knowledge more accessible, and build on the values he devoted his life to.
